## 2. Scope of Work — Lead Risk Assessment

Conducted to **HUD Guidelines (Ch. 5 — Risk Assessment)**, **EPA 40 CFR Part 745**, **Ohio Administrative Code 3701-32**, and formatted for **City of Cleveland Lead-Safe Certification** submission.

**Included:**

- Owner / occupant interview and use-pattern questionnaire
- Visual assessment of every painted interior and exterior component
- Dust wipe sampling — floors, window sills, and window troughs (per ASTM E1728)
- Paint-chip sampling on any deteriorated painted surface
- Soil sampling at bare-soil locations (drip line, play area if applicable)
- Chain-of-custody submission to **EHS Analytical** (AIHA-LAP accredited, Account #202623)
- Photo-documented, **component-level** report — every tested surface individually reported
- Prioritized remediation guidance (interim controls vs. abatement) ranked by hazard severity
- City-submission-ready packet (HEA 7729, lab results, photos, diagrams)
- Post-delivery support — 1 follow-up call to walk you through findings & next steps

**Not included (available as add-ons or separate services):**

- Full XRF Lead-Based Paint Inspection (for 20-Year Exemption) — see §6
- Lead Clearance Exam after remediation — see §6
- Remediation / abatement work itself (Guardian inspects, does not abate)
